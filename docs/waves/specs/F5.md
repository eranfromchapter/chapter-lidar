# F5 Photos and field controls (iOS)

## Summary

Collects the evidence the guide says cannot be recovered later: labeled site photographs and
independent field measurements, and blocks "finish capture" until the before-leaving checklist
passes. Photos are taken with the app's own camera so Chapter keeps the originals.

## In scope

- Photo capture per room with required labels: room, viewing direction (N/E/S/W or "toward
  <wall/landmark>"), subject (opening, ceiling change, cabinetry, fixture, equipment, defect,
  general). Original HEIC/JPEG kept unmodified with EXIF; a privacy reminder to avoid personal items.
- Field-controls entry that writes `field-controls.json`: two long perpendicular distances per
  floor, ceiling heights, opening dimensions, floor-to-floor level, shared landmarks between
  captures. Units chosen per entry (mm, m, ft-in) and stored in mm. Optional Bluetooth laser input
  is out of v1; manual entry only.
- Before-leaving checklist (pure Swift rules in target `FieldRules`, Linux-testable): every floor
  labeled; omitted/inaccessible rooms recorded; ≥ 1 photo per captured room; the controls minimum
  set present per floor; device/mode/date/operator present.
- Controls minimum set (approved by Eran 2026-10-17): per floor, long distances A and B plus at least one
  ceiling height; between each pair of consecutive floors, one floor-to-floor level and two shared landmarks;
  openings optional.
- UI screens for photos, controls and checklist, per the frozen design.
- Design v2 (Oct 21, 2026, artboard 2d, phone-field spec): Photos ("Label a photo" with "Looking
  toward" pre-filled from the camera's compass bearing when the reading is good, else chosen by
  hand), Field controls (feet and inches typed as two fields, ASCII marks, comma thousands),
  Before you leave (blocking items only, in list order, with the v2 wording; the compass item is
  shown and never blocks), renaming rooms from More and from the checklist, and a read-only mode
  after sealing. FieldKitUI is drawn with ChapterUI (Inter, Times display titles).
- Manifest 1.1.0: every photo entry records `photo.directionSource` (`compass` or `manual`).
- Field controls policy (owner question Q-A): `ChecklistPolicy.standard` ships, so the scan's
  values are enough and tape or laser controls are optional; the manual policy of the drawings
  ("N of M required") is built behind one constant (`ChecklistPolicy.shipping`).

## Out of scope

- Uploading (F6). Accuracy comparison (F8 does it at the station).
