# Chapter brand assets

`chapter-logo.svg` and the fonts are generated by `tools/brand/generate_plan_brand.py`; do not
edit them by hand.

- `chapter-logo.svg`: the Chapter logo, as published on hellochapter.com. A trademark of Chapter;
  not covered by any open license.
- `inter-400.woff2`, `inter-600.woff2`: Inter Regular and SemiBold, Latin subset.
  Copyright 2020 The Inter Project Authors (https://github.com/rsms/inter). Licensed under the
  SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). The copyright and license
  fields are kept in each font's name table.
- `chapter-wordmark.svg`: the chapter wordmark used in the HUB (HelloChapter backoffice
  `public/images/logo.svg`, via the HUB design system). A trademark of Chapter; not covered by any
  open license. Copied by hand from `tools/brand/chapter-wordmark.svg`.
